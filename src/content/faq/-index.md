description: "Common questions about Cayto Group's managed accounting service and offshore staffing model — scope, pricing, security, and how engagements work."

groups:
  - label: "Managed Accounting"
    faqs:
      - title: What exactly is included?
        answer: "Every engagement includes the full accounting cycle: transaction categorization, bank and credit card and balance sheet reconciliations, accounts payable, accounts receivable, month-end close entries, monthly financial statements, a management reporting package, and a tax-ready year-end handoff to your CPA. Scope does not change between pricing tiers — only volume does."

      - title: How is it priced?
        answer: "Flat monthly, based on transaction volume. Under 100 transactions per month is $500. Between 100 and 250 is $750. Above 250 is $1,000. A transaction is a bank or credit card line item categorized in that month. We size your tier from your actual statements during the assessment and tell you the price before we start."

      - title: Do I have to change accounting systems?
        answer: "Managed Accounting runs on the Caytava platform by default, and we handle the setup and migration for you. If you are already on a system you need to keep, tell us during the assessment and we will tell you honestly whether we can work in it."

      - title: What is Caytava, and why is it billed separately?
        answer: "Caytava is an AI-native accounting platform built by our sister company — same founding and management team, separate legal entity. Cayto Group delivers all services, including implementation, migration, bookkeeping, close, and reporting. Caytava provides the platform, which you subscribe to directly. It typically costs less than what companies pay today for QuickBooks or Xero."

      - title: Is AI doing my accounting?
        answer: "AI does the mechanical work: capturing and coding documents and feeds, matching transactions, keeping accounts reconciled, and flagging anomalies for review. It does not make accounting decisions and it does not post to your books on its own. A qualified Cayto accountant reviews and approves every entry before it becomes a record."

      - title: Who actually does the work, and who do I talk to?
        answer: "A Cayto delivery team handles your books, and you have one named point of contact on that team for day-to-day questions. A U.S.-based Cayto principal is accountable for the engagement. Our professionals hold recognized accounting credentials, over 70% have Big 4 experience, and all of them pass reference checks, prior employer verification, and full background screening before touching client work."

      - title: My books are months behind. Can you fix that?
        answer: "Yes, and it is common. Cleanup is billed at your normal monthly rate for each month we bring current, so there is no separate project fee to negotiate. We will tell you during the assessment roughly how far back the work goes and how long it will take."

      - title: Do you file my taxes?
        answer: "No. We keep your books tax-ready year-round and deliver a clean package to your CPA at year-end, which is where most of the cost and pain of tax season actually comes from. We do not prepare or file returns."

      - title: How long does it take to get started?
        answer: "Assessment, then setup and migration, then your first close. Most engagements are in steady state within a few weeks. Books that need significant catching up take longer, and we will tell you the realistic timeline up front rather than after you have signed."

      - title: How do I pay, and am I locked in?
        answer: "You receive a monthly invoice from Cayto Group LLC, a U.S.-based limited liability company, and we provide a W-9. The initial term is three months, then month to month. You retain full ownership of your financial data and work product."

  - label: "Dedicated Professionals"
    faqs:
      - title: How are Cayto Group resources priced?
        answer: We seek to staff clients with full-time resources at roughly half the cost of hiring someone locally in the United States. The cost varies depending on the position being filled, but our quoted costs are fully burdened costs. We do not bill extra for overtime and clients are not required to provide benefits.

      - title: How do I pay the resource?
        answer: Clients receive a monthly invoice from Cayto Group LLC, which is a US-based limited liability company. Cayto Group provides a W-9 and invoices clients monthly based on a fixed monthly rate for each resource. The resources staffed to clients are compensated directly by Cayto Group and its affiliates.

      - title: What if I am unhappy with the resource that is staffed?
        answer: Cayto Group offers a 14-day guarantee for all resources that are staffed to clients. If a client is unsatisfied with a resource, it can cancel the contract for the resource within the first 14 days and not be charged for the resource.

      - title: What level of accounting resources does Cayto Group staff?
        answer: Cayto Group supplies clients with exclusive full-time accounting resources in senior, staff, and specialist (including accounts receivable and accounts payable) roles. Certain clients have promoted our senior accountants to accounting managers, but we don't seek to staff those positions initially.

      - title: What happens if a client promotes a Cayto Group resource?
        answer: Cayto Group will seek to back-fill the promoted resource, and the new resource staffed by Cayto Group can be trained, in part, by the promoted client resource.

      - title: What is the time zone difference between CG resources and the USA?
        answer: Our professionals are between 10–14 hours ahead of the USA depending on the client's time zone and whether daylight savings is in effect. Cayto Group staff adjust working hours to have 4–5 hours of overlap during the initial onboarding and training process, and understand they will be required to take calls in the evenings to join team meetings and speak with supervisors in the United States on an ongoing basis.

      - title: How do I communicate with Cayto Group resources and what IT security measures are in place?
        answer: We work with your company's IT department to provide Cayto Group resources with communication accounts (Slack, Skype, Teams, etc.) used by your other team members. Our resources adopt your company's IT protocols the same as if they were hired directly by your company. If your company does not have IT protocols that specifically address all of the needs of the situation, Cayto Group offers solutions at no additional cost, such as a secure file-sharing portal with multi-factor authentication and password management solutions.

      - title: Which service line is right for my company?
        answer: If you have a Controller, VP of Finance, or CFO leading accounting and you need more capacity, staffing is the fit — you get a dedicated professional who works under your direction. If nobody is leading accounting and you want the books handled entirely, Managed Accounting is the fit. If you are not sure, tell us on a call and we will give you a straight answer, including when the answer is neither.
